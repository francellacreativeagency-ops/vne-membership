## REEL 1 — "The Same Fight" · Awareness (~35s)

**[TEXT: You're not having 100 fights. You're having ONE fight 100 times.]**

**[P1]** If you and your partner keep having the same argument over and over…
**[P2]** …just wearing different clothes each time —
**[P1]** it's not because one of you is difficult.
**[P2]** And it's not a communication problem.
**[P1]** It's a *wiring* problem. The two of you are literally built to process love,
stress, and conflict differently —
**[P2]** and no one ever handed you the manual.
**[BOTH]** So you're stuck in the gap: the fight you keep having, and the closeness you
actually want.
**[P1]** The good news? That gap is measurable. And once you can see it —
**[P2]** you can close it.
**[TEXT / CTA: Follow. Part 2 → why you can't see it yourselves.]**

## FOUNDER STORY — 15-min interview (question set)

_Interviewer asks; the couple answers conversationally. Goal: capture the raw "heart"
video + pull 3–5 micro-clips. The through-line: **they lived the gap and crossed it.**_

**Warm-up (1–2 min)**
1. Tell me who you two are, and who you help.
2. Before any of the tools or frameworks — who were you two as a couple at your lowest point?

**The gap they lived (the emotional core — spend the most time here, 5–6 min)**
3. Take me to the moment it almost fell apart. What was actually happening?
4. What were you telling yourselves back then? Did either of you think you'd picked the
   wrong person?
5. What was the fight you kept having — and why couldn't you solve it no matter how hard
   you tried?

**The turn (3–4 min)**
6. What did you discover about how you're each wired that changed everything?
7. What's the moment you realized "it was never a compatibility problem"?
8. When did you know you had to build this into something you could give other couples?

**The bridge / their work (2–3 min)**
9. What does the relationship gauge actually do for a couple — in plain terms?
10. What's the transformation you watch couples go through?
11. Tell me about one couple whose story still gets you. What changed for them?

**The invitation (1 min)**
12. For the couple watching this right now, quietly wondering if it's just them — what
    do you want them to know?
13. What's the very first step they should take today?

**Pinned-post intro (record as a clean 20–30s standalone):**
_"We build tools for couples' relationships. Here's the fight that almost ended ours —
and what it taught us. Watch this before you decide your relationship is 'just hard.'"_
