A **couple** who coaches **couples** to understand **the science of how they're wired**.

## Offer diagnostic (the Monday review)

### The false belief (what keeps couples stuck)
> _"If our relationship is this hard, something must be wrong with us / we might not be
> compatible."_ — They believe love should be effortless, and struggle = a verdict on
> the relationship.

**The reframe (their unique mechanism):** It's not a compatibility problem — it's a
**wiring problem you were never taught to read.** You're not broken; you're running two
different operating systems with no manual. The science explains it, and the gauge shows
you exactly where the friction is.

### The pain (surface symptoms couples actually name)
- Having the **same fight on repeat** with no resolution.
- Feeling like **roommates**, not partners — logistics replaced intimacy.
- One feels **unheard**; the other feels **criticized / like they can't win**.
- **Walking on eggshells** to avoid the blow-up.
- Quiet, scary thought: _"Is this just how it's going to be?"_

### The desire (the outcome on the other side)
- Feeling **deeply understood** and chosen again.
- Knowing **why** they clash — and having the **tools** to navigate it in real time.
- A relationship that feels like a **team**, not two people negotiating a truce.
- Confidence that they can handle the next hard season **without it threatening the union.**

### Is the offer irresistible? (honest critique)
Strong bones, three fixes to make it a no-brainer:
1. **Lead with the gauge, not the 17 pages.** "17-page document" sounds like homework.
   "A relationship gauge that shows your 3 biggest blindspots in 10 minutes" sounds like
   a mirror you can't resist looking into. The doc is the deliverable; the **gauge is the hook.**
2. **Name the specific transformation + timeframe.** Vague "improve your relationship" →
   "stop having the same fight within 30 days by seeing the wiring underneath it."
3. **Make the assessment a standalone lead magnet.** Free/low-ticket gauge → results call →
   paid program. The blindspots it reveals ARE the sales conversation.

> **Back-end upsell fit:** this couple is a prime **Offer Architect** candidate — turn the
> 17-page doc + assessment into a clean ladder (free gauge → paid program → continuity),
> then **build the landing page** around the gauge as the conversion centerpiece.

## The 5 reels (threaded with widen-the-gap)

### Reel 1 — "The Same Fight" · Awareness
- **Hook:** _"If you keep having the same fight, it's not a communication problem. It's a
  wiring problem."_
- **Gap move:** name the symptom (the loop) → reveal the invisible gap (two different
  wirings) → hint the gauge makes it visible.
- **CTA:** follow / "part 2: why you can't see it yourself."

### Reel 2 — "You're Not Incompatible" · Engagement (belief shift)
- **Hook:** _"Struggling doesn't mean you picked the wrong person. It means nobody handed
  you the manual."_
- **Gap move:** break the false belief head-on; reframe struggle as an unread manual, not
  a verdict. Widens the gap between "we're doomed" (their fear) and "we're just un-manualed"
  (the truth).
- **CTA:** comment / save — "tag your partner."

### Reel 3 — "The Other Side" · Engagement (desire/transformation)
- **Hook:** _"Here's what changes when a couple finally sees their wiring…"_
- **Gap move:** paint the desired outcome vividly (team, understood, same fight gone). Use
  a real client arc if available.
- **CTA:** "want to see yours? →"

### Reel 4 — "The Gauge / What's Inside" · Conversion-setup
- **Hook:** _"You can't fix a blindspot you can't see. So we built a gauge for your
  relationship."_
- **Gap move:** the assessment + framework = the bridge. Show it measures exactly where the
  gap is and hands you the steps to close it.
- **CTA:** "here's how to take it →"

### Reel 5 — "Take the Gauge" · Conversion
- **Hook:** _"In 10 minutes, you'll see the 3 blindspots quietly driving your biggest
  fights — and what to do about each one."_
- **Gap move:** stay in the fog (symptoms) or see the map (outcome). Direct ask + timing.
- **CTA (hard):** take the assessment / book the results call.
