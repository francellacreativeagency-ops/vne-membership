## The one-line promise

**Come with your offer. Leave with the content, story, proof, and strategy to sell it.**

We are not selling five hours in a studio. We are selling a **strategic content
production system** that takes a coach from _"I know I need content"_ to _"I have
the assets and the strategy to actually market my offer."_

## The philosophy (the spine of everything)

- **Going viral is not a marketing strategy.** We create **conversion content**,
  not viral content.
- Every piece of content has **a job to do**: move the right person one **micro-yes**
  closer to buying.
- 500 views from the exact people who need what you sell beats 100,000 views from
  people who never will.
- **The core through-line for ALL content:** _widen the gap between the client's
  present symptoms and their desired outcome._ Name where they are now, make the
  distance to where they want to be vivid, then position the offer as the bridge.
  (This message must appear in every reel and the Original Story — see
  `03-reel-content-strategy.md`.)

Content should answer: _Do I trust you? Do you understand my problem? Can you
actually help me? What would my life look like if I worked with you? Why you?_

## The 3-phase system

### PHASE 1 — PREP · "Content Strategy Kickoff"
**Monday, September 7 · Online · 60–75 min group Zoom**
Not a "prep meeting" — the Kickoff.

> **✅ CRITICAL INTAKE REQUIREMENT:** Every coach must submit a **link to their offer /
> landing page well ahead of Monday** (build it into booking — see below). Francis
> reviews each one in advance so the Kickoff can sharpen: the **false belief**, the
> **pain**, the **desire**, and whether the **offer itself is irresistible**. Reviewing
> live on the call is too late — the pre-work is what makes the strategy land.

Coaches submit their offer, funnel, and business info **before** the call so Francis
reviews funnels in advance. On the call we work through each coach's **Content Map**:

- My offer / I help… / The problem I solve / The transformation
- My primary CTA
- Three things my audience must believe before buying
- My founder (origin) story
- My best client transformation
- Five reel topics
- What to bring, what to wear, their individual Saturday schedule
- Whether they're bringing a client for a testimonial

Benefit: catches problems before the studio ("your landing page sells three things,"
"you don't know your founder story," "you don't know what reels to make").

### PHASE 2 — PRODUCTION · "Content That Converts Intensive"
**Saturday, September 12 · 8 AM–1 PM · Vinestudio158**
They arrive already prepared. Saturday is **CREATE. CREATE. CREATE.** — not
Think → Decide → Create → Get distracted. See `02-deliverables-per-coach.md` and
the run-of-day below.

### PHASE 3 — DEPLOYMENT · "Content Deployment Plan"
The "golden hour." Everyone leaves knowing: _What content do I have? What does each
piece promote? What CTA? Where does it go? What do I post first? What becomes an ad?_
