# Reel Content Strategy — The 5 Reels + Original Story

_This is the answer to: "What 5 pieces of content actually drive traffic and convert
into a program?" Reconciles the different reel counts from Francis's voice note into
one coherent, recommended set. **[DRAFT — refine with the details Francis is sending.]**_

---

## The through-line (non-negotiable, in EVERY piece)

**Widen the gap between the client's present symptoms and their desired outcome.**

Every reel and the Original Story must, somewhere, do this move:
1. **Name the symptom** — the surface-level pain they feel and complain about now.
2. **Name the desired outcome** — the specific life/result on the other side.
3. **Make the gap vivid** — the cost of staying where they are; the size of the leap.
4. **Position the offer as the bridge** — the mechanism that closes that gap.

> Symptoms are what they _think_ the problem is. The desired outcome is what they
> actually want. Great conversion content sells the gap between them — then names
> the program as the only clean way across.

---

## Reconciling the counts

Francis floated three versions:
- "One of each": awareness / engagement / conversion (3)
- "Two of each" then "2 awareness, 2 engagement, 1 conversion" (5)
- A separate 5 "toward the offer": agitate the painful problem · the program (what's
  in it) · when it's happening · belief systems (×3) · painful problems

**Recommendation: 5 reels, each mapped to BOTH a function (awareness/engagement/
conversion) AND an offer job.** This satisfies both framings at once — the mix stays
2 awareness / 2 engagement / 1 conversion, and all 5 point at the offer.

## The recommended 5 reels

### Reel 1 — "The Painful Problem" · Awareness
**Job:** Name the daily symptom out loud so the right person feels seen.
- Hook on the symptom they'd actually say ("You keep _____ but still feel _____").
- **Gap move:** here's the symptom → here's what you actually want → the distance
  between them isn't your fault, it's a missing bridge.
- CTA (soft): follow / "part 2 shows the real cause."

### Reel 2 — "The Belief Shift" · Engagement
**Job:** Break the #1 false belief keeping them stuck (this absorbs the "belief
systems they need to adopt" — pick the single most load-bearing belief; the other
beliefs become future content, not separate reels here).
- Contrarian / confrontational open (matches the brand's "going viral isn't a
  strategy" energy).
- **Gap move:** "You think the gap is [X]. It's actually [Y]." Reframes why they're
  stuck at the symptom and can't reach the outcome.
- CTA: comment / save — sparks the engagement signal.

### Reel 3 — "The Transformation" · Engagement
**Job:** Paint the desired outcome vividly and prove it's reachable.
- Show the "other side" — what life looks like once the gap is closed (a client, a
  before/after, a day-in-the-life).
- **Gap move:** anchors hard on the _desired outcome_ end of the gap so the audience
  can "see themselves on the other side of it."
- CTA: "want the path? here's how →" (bridges to Reel 4/5).

### Reel 4 — "The Program / What's In It" · Conversion-setup
**Job:** Reveal the mechanism — what the program is and what's inside — as the bridge.
- What it is, what's included, the specific way it closes the gap (mechanism, not
  feature dump).
- **Gap move:** explicitly map each part of the program to the step it removes
  between symptom and outcome.
- CTA: "here's who it's for and when it opens →" (sets up Reel 5).

### Reel 5 — "The Offer + When It's Happening" · Conversion
**Job:** Make the ask. Who it's for, what they get, urgency/timing, the CTA.
- Direct offer, scarcity/timing ("when it's happening"), and one clear call to action.
- **Gap move:** "Stay here (symptoms) or cross now (outcome). The door is open until ___."
- CTA (hard): link / DM / apply.

**Mix check:** Awareness (1, 2*) · Engagement (2*, 3) · Conversion (4→5). Reel 2 bridges
awareness↔engagement. This gives the 2/2/1 shape while every reel serves the offer.

> If Francis wants to honor the literal "three belief-system reels," swap Reel 3 →
> a mini belief-series (3 shorter beliefs) and treat Transformation as part of Reel 5.
> Recommendation stands: **one sharp belief reel beats three thin ones.**

## The Original Story + Framework (the 6th piece — the "heart")

- Long-form **interview** (10–15 Q's, Francis's template/framework) → the emotional
  core of the whole content library.
- **Gap move at scale:** the founder's own journey _is_ the gap story — where they
  started (their symptoms), what they crossed, and why they built the bridge for others.
- Ships with a **pinned post** that introduces the Original Story on the profile.
- Can be repurposed into: the podcast episode, multiple micro-reels, email sequence,
  and the "about" section of the funnel.
