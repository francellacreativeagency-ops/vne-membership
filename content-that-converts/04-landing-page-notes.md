# Landing Page — Alignment Notes

_From the review in the uploaded doc. These are the edits that make the sales promise
match the actual experience (7 coaches, 2 rooms, thin walls). Captured for later._

1. **Make Monday a formal part of the experience.** Elevate it from "script writing"
   to **THE CONTENT STRATEGY KICKOFF** (Mon, Sept 7 · Online): review offer, review
   funnel/landing page, identify what the audience must believe before buying, develop
   content angles, write reel scripts, prep founder story, choose podcast topic,
   confirm testimonial, walk through the individual Saturday schedule.
   > _"You don't show up Saturday wondering what we're going to create. You show up
   > ready to create it."_

2. **Clarify the three stations** into what they actually mean:
   - 📸 **Station 1 — Brand Photo** (images for site, social, launches, email, sales)
   - 🎥 **Station 2 — Reels** (teleprompter ready, scripts from Monday, coaching from Francis)
   - 🎙️ **Station 3 — Authority Content** (authority conversation + founder story)

3. **Change the headline promise.** Avoid "Get a Full Quarter of Content." Use:
   **"Get a Content Library Built Around Your Offer in One Saturday Morning."**
   Sub: _"Stop creating content just to stay visible. Create the content your audience
   needs to see before they say yes."_

4. **Name the four content pillars** (Content Ecosystem): 01 Visibility (photos+reels) ·
   02 Authority (podcast) · 03 Connection (founder story) · 04 Proof (client testimonial,
   optional).

5. **Move the funnel review earlier** — it's the strongest differentiator. Frame the
   process: _What are we selling? → Who are we talking to? → What must they believe? →
   What content builds that belief? → Produce it._ (Francis the strategist, not studio owner.)

6. **Reframe the Saturday flow** on the page (don't advertise 3 loud things at once):
   Welcome + Brief → Production Block 1 (photos/reels/prep) → Production Block 2
   (podcast/interviews) → Final Capture + Content Check.

7. **Expand the Monday "Script Lab"** →
   _Offer → Audience → Problems → Transformation → Story → Proof → Content → CTA._
   > _"Your job on Saturday isn't to figure out what to say. Your job is to say it."_

8. **Keep the honesty** — _"There are no testimonials from this room yet."_ Founding
   cohort framing justifies the $297 rate. Don't remove it.

9. **Tighten the final offer** into YOU BRING / WE PROVIDE / YOU LEAVE WITH (see
   `01-master-brief.md`), and add a **"Your prep starts before you arrive"** section
   connecting sale → onboarding → Monday prep → Saturday production into one journey.

## Contrarian hook (keep — it's the brand voice)
> **Going viral is not a marketing strategy.** … I don't care how many people saw your
> video. I care how many people moved **closer to saying yes.** … You don't need more
> random content. You need content with a job to do.
