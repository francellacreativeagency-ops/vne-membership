# Film Scripts — Hero + Closing (read aloud by Francis)

_VSL-style scripts for the two landing-page film placeholders. Strategy: widen the gap —
name their pain better than they can, show the deliverables, paint the ideal state, widen
the gap again, then close with urgency (7 seats, first come first served, founding price
before it rises). Read conversational, direct, warm-but-confrontational._

---

## HERO FILM (~50–60 seconds)

Let me describe your last ninety days.

You know your offer can change someone's life — you've watched it happen. But your
content? Crickets. You post, you second-guess it, you delete it, you disappear for two
weeks. You're sitting on a transformation people would gladly pay thousands for… and it's
invisible.

And it's not because you're lazy. You don't have the studio, the gear, the strategy, or
the time. So you keep promising yourself you'll "batch content this weekend." You never do.
Meanwhile someone with half your expertise is booking your dream clients — just because
they're *seen.*

Here's the truth: going viral was never your problem. **Being forgettable is.**

So on September 12th, seven coaches walk into The Vine Studio with nothing but an offer —
and five hours later they walk out with **ten brand photos, five reels built to convert, a
founder-story film, behind-the-scenes footage, all their raw files, and a plan for exactly
what to post and when.** We'll even sit with you, edit your first piece right inside
Instagram, TikTok or Facebook, and help you **schedule your first post before you leave.**

No blank camera. No "what do I even say." You show up prepared — we produce — and you leave
with a content library engineered to move the right people one **yes** closer.

But here's the part you need to hear: there are only **seven seats.** One studio, one
Saturday, **first come, first served.** And because this is the very first time Vine Studio
has ever opened this — before there are testimonials on the wall — you get it at the
founding price. Once the results come in, this price is gone for good.

**Come with your offer. Leave with the content to sell it.** Tap the link and grab your
seat before it's taken.

---

## CLOSING FILM (~25–30 seconds)

So here's the decision in front of you.

You can spend another quarter posting into the void — hoping the algorithm finally notices
you, watching the gap between where you are and where you want to be get *wider.*

Or you can block one Saturday, walk in with your offer, and walk out with everything you
need to actually sell it — the photos, the reels, your story, and the exact strategy behind
them, with your first post already scheduled.

**Seven seats. First come, first served. Founding price — before it goes up.**

Stop chasing views. Start creating content with a job to do.

Grab your seat. I'll see you in the studio.

---

## Delivery notes
- **Hero:** shoot to camera, one take feel. Cut B-roll over the middle third (the
  deliverables list) so the assets flash on screen as you name them.
- **Closing:** tighter framing, slower pace, direct eye contact on "I'll see you in the studio."
- Both end on the same CTA energy so the button below the video feels like the next step.
- Swap in the live booking link and confirm the seat count is current before recording.
