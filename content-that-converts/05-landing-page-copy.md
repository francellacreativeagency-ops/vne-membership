## SECTION 2 — THE MANIFESTO (propaganda / belief shift)

**If you're building a personal brand to sell coaching, I don't care how many people saw your video.**

I care how many people moved **closer to saying yes.**

100,000 views from people who'll never buy from you is not better than 500 views from
the exact people who need what you sell. Your content should answer five questions:

- Do I trust you?
- Do you understand my problem?
- Can you actually help me?
- What would my life look like if I worked with you?
- Why should I choose you?

**You don't need more random content. You need content with a job to do.**

## SECTION 3 — THE TRANSFORMATION (Myron — who you become)

**You're not booking a studio. You're changing what you're able to sell.**

Most coaches undercharge because they think they're selling trinkets, their time, or
their training. You're not. You're selling a **measurable, desirable transformation** —
and the person your client becomes on the other side of yes has a higher quality of life
than the person who walked in.

**Your content has to sell that gap.** The distance between the symptoms your audience
feels today and the outcome they actually want. That's the one job every piece of content
you make here is built to do: **widen that gap until saying yes becomes the obvious move.**

Walk out able to charge what your transformation is actually worth — because now you have
the content to prove it.
