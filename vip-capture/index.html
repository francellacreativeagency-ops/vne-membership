House rules for a premium look
  • Expose for faces, protect highlights. Stage spots will blow skin first. Highlight alert on.
  • Shoot through something. Layer foreground blur (shoulders, candles, florals) for depth. That's what separates premium from snapshots.
  • Clean frames. Move one step to lose exit signs, cables, AV cases and water bottles.
  • Wide every 20 minutes. A full room is the best advert we have.
  • Pair every speaker shot with a reaction from the same moment.
  • Video: a 40/60 mix. 40% horizontal 4K, 60% vertical for reels. Horizontal is for YouTube, the session films and the room wides. Vertical is for every reel moment: worship, soundbites, walk-ins, cake, dance floor, BTS. Shoot vertical natively (gimbal in vertical mode or an L-bracket); don't count on cropping. When a moment matters for both, get 10–15 s horizontal, then rotate and get it again vertical.

Posing for a premium look
  • Shoulders angled 45°, weight on the back foot, chin slightly forward and down.
  • Full length: camera at hip height, which makes her taller and more elegant. Close-ups: camera just above eye level.
  • Give her something to do: walk toward you, adjust an earring, look back over her shoulder.
  • Get the real smile by making her laugh (S2 can help). The second frame after the laugh is usually the one.
  • Guests: one posed frame looking at camera, one candid laugh, done in 20 seconds. Amari notes names against shot numbers.

Handle with care

Worship, prayer & presbytery

Etiquette
  • No flash. Silent shutter (electronic) during prayer. If you see banding from the LEDs, switch back to mechanical and shoot between quiet moments.
  • Shoot from the edges, back and balcony. Never walk down the centre aisle or stand between the minister and the person being prayed for.
  • Use the 70-200 and the 85 so you can stay back. Hands and silhouettes are beautiful and keep people private.
  • Presbytery: confirm with Dr. Faith or the organiser that photos are welcome. Shoot visuals only, never record the audio of a personal word. Stop if anyone waves you off.
  • Music rights: most worship songs are copyrighted. Record it, but plan licensed or ambient audio for IG and YouTube unless the team owns the song.
